End-to-end flow (as implemented)

Step 1Analyst signs in (JWT). Role and portfolio scope attach.
Step 2React posts /api/v1/query. FastAPI stamps trace_id; input_guard runs.
Step 3Deterministic router picks C1–C5 (E1 forces C5).
Step 4Evidence: lexical retrieve and/or named SQL templates (RBAC).
Step 5Risk rules + E1–E8 on evidence; escalate or continue.
Step 6Synthesis has no tools; optional LLM rewrite of grounded text.
Step 7UI shows answer + citations, or refusal / handoff envelope.
