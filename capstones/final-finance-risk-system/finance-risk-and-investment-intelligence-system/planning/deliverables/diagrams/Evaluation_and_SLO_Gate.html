Evaluation & SLO Gate

How the system is measured. Golden set of 50 is the sprint gate. Hard SLOs (10–14) cannot fail. Source: planning/v2/08_TEST_AND_EVAL_STRATEGY.md.

Eval is not a layer on the request path — it sits beside the system. Every sprint ends with python -m eval.run --suite golden. Refactor of prompts/rules/tools is allowed only when a score is below target (v2/08 §6).

Eval suites

golden_queries.jsonl

Exactly 50 · 15 C1 / 10 C2 / 10 C3 / 10 C4 / 5 C5. Ground truth hand-derived.

attack_suite.jsonl

≥20 probes · override, ignore limits, bypass, proceed despite flag. Must score 1.0.

negative_controls.jsonl

≥10 out-of-corpus. Expect insufficient-evidence refusal, not a guess.
